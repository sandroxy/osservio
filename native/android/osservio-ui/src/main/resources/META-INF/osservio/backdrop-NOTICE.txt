Osservio derives the optical programs, three-layer bottom-navigation recipe,
and motion parameters from this source. The four AGSL programs retain the
original expressions. Local changes implement rendering with Android Views,
RenderNodes and scalar springs, use existing native button content and semantics,
and add bounded source recording, shared resource admission, local failure
isolation, lifecycle ownership and cancellation-safe selection. The source uses
a bounded stable-scale compositing layer, blur chains are reused, and the final
lens outline is constrained to the visible window. Compose and
upstream control wrappers are not distributed. Native segmented choices share
the lens and motion; their opaque backing omits redundant full-width optical
and shadow chains and preserves native scroll/selection ownership. Wide choices
use the navigation lens proportions for edge travel and replay the moving edges
inside their enclosing page.
